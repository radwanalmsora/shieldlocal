# Scan your workspace

ShieldLocal checks every supported file for:

- 🔑 **Secrets** – cloud keys, tokens, private keys, passwords
- 💉 **Injection** – SQL, command, XSS, unsafe deserialisation
- 🔐 **Crypto & TLS** – weak hashes, disabled verification
- 📦 **Dependencies** – known-vulnerable npm and PyPI packages

Nothing is uploaded. All analysis runs on your machine.
